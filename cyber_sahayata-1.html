What to do now

First response checklist

  1. Secure the email account linked to your social media.
  2. Use the official recovery page from a device you normally use.
  3. Check email for security alerts about changed passwords or contact details.
  4. After recovery, change the password and sign out unfamiliar sessions.
  5. Turn on two-factor authentication and save backup codes securely.
  6. Tell friends not to send money or click links from the compromised account.
